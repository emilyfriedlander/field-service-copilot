## 2. Van Stock — Required Inventory by Trade

### HVAC Van Stock (Minimum)
- Capacitors: 5µF, 7.5µF, 10µF, 35µF, 45µF, 55µF (2 each)
- Contactors: 1-pole 30A, 2-pole 30A, 2-pole 40A (2 each)
- Thermostat: 1x smart (Ecobee or Honeywell T6), 1x standard
- Drain line tablets (1 box), P-trap (2x)
- Refrigerant gauges + manifold set
- R-410A recovery cylinder (must be returned when < 5 lb)
- Air filters: 1", 2", 4" common sizes (2 each)
- Blower motor run capacitors (assorted)
- Flame sensors (2x universal)

### Plumbing Van Stock (Minimum)
- Shut-off valves: 1/2", 3/4", 1" (3 each)
- Wax rings (4x), toilet bolts (2 packs)
- P-traps: 1.5", 1.25" (2 each)
- Supply lines: braided steel, assorted lengths (6x)
- Pipe cutter, torch, solder, flux
- PVC fittings: elbows, couplings, tees — 1.5", 2", 3" (3 each)
- Teflon tape (5 rolls), pipe dope (1 tube)
- Water heater expansion tank (2x)
- TPR valve — 3/4" (2x)
- Flapper kits (4x), fill valves (2x)

### Electrical Van Stock (Minimum)
- Breakers: 15A single, 20A single, 15A double, 20A double, 30A double (2 each)
- GFCI outlets: 15A, 20A (4 each)
- Standard outlets + switches (10 each)
- Wire connectors: assorted (1 box)
- 12/2 NM-B wire (50 ft), 14/2 NM-B wire (50 ft)
- Conduit: 1/2" EMT (10 ft), LB fitting (2x)
- Voltage tester, multimeter, non-contact tester
- Wire labels and panel directory cards
- Junction boxes: 1-gang, 2-gang, 4" round (4 each)

## 3. Warranty Reference

| Equipment Type        | Parts Warranty | Labor Warranty | Registration Required? |
|-----------------------|----------------|----------------|------------------------|
| AC / Heat Pump        | 10 years       | 1 year         | Yes — within 60 days   |
| Furnace               | 20 years (HX), 5 yr parts | 1 year | Yes — within 60 days |
| Water Heater (tank)   | 6–10 years     | 1 year         | Yes — within 30 days   |
| Tankless Water Heater | 15 years (HX), 5 yr parts | 1 year | Yes — within 30 days |
| Electrical Panel      | Manufacturer varies | 1 year    | No                     |
| Generator (standby)   | 5 years        | 1 year         | Yes — within 30 days   |
| Installed parts/repair| 30 days        | 30 days        | No                     |

**Technician responsibility:** Register all equipment with the manufacturer via the vendor
portal before closing the job. Failure to register voids the extended warranty and is a
disciplinable offense.
