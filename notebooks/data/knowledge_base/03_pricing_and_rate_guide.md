# Pricing & Rate Guide
**Apex Home Services — Internal Operations Document**
*Effective: January 1, 2024 | Owner: Finance & Operations*

---

## 1. Labor Rates

| Technician Level | Standard Rate ($/hr) | After-Hours Rate ($/hr) |
|------------------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| Junior           | $75                  | $110                    |
| Mid-Level        | $95                  | $135                    |
| Senior           | $125                 | $175                    |

- Minimum labor charge: **1 hour** per job visit.
- Travel time is **not** billed separately for jobs within our service zones.
- Jobs exceeding 8 hours require Service Manager pre-approval for overtime billing.

## 4. Parts Markup Policy

| Parts Cost (Cost to Us) | Customer Markup |
|-------------------------|-----------------|
| $0 – $50                | 100%            |
| $51 – $200              | 75%             |
| $201 – $500             | 50%             |
| $501+                   | 35%             |

- Markup policy is **internal only** — never share with customers.
- All parts must be sourced through approved vendors; no personal sourcing.

## 5. Discounts & Promotions

| Program                    | Discount           | Applies To                         |
|----------------------------|--------------------|------------------------------------|
| Club Membership (annual)   | 15% off labor      | All service calls                  |
| Senior Citizen (65+)       | 10% off labor      | Standard jobs, not emergencies     |
| Military / First Responder | 10% off labor      | With valid ID                      |
| Referral Credit            | $50 off next visit | Applied to referring customer only |

## 6. Financing Options

- **12-month same-as-cash** available on jobs ≥ $500 (GreenSky partner).
- **60-month low-interest** available on jobs ≥ $1,500.
- Technician initiates financing application in the app; approval is instant in most cases.
- Financing is available for residential customers only.
