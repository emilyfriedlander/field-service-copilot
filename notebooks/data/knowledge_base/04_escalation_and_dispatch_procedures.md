# Escalation & Dispatch Procedures
**Apex Home Services — Internal Operations Document**
*Effective: January 1, 2024 | Owner: Dispatch & Service Operations*

---

## 1. Dispatch Flow Overview

```
Customer Call / Online Request
        ↓
Dispatcher creates Work Order in system
        ↓
Priority assigned (Emergency / High / Medium / Low)
        ↓
Nearest available qualified tech assigned
        ↓
Tech notified via app → must acknowledge within 15 min
        ↓
Tech dispatched → en route update sent to customer
        ↓
Job completed → closed in app → CSAT survey triggered
```

---

## 2. Dispatcher Responsibilities

- Monitor the live dispatch board at all times during shift.
- Assign jobs based on: trade match, proximity, current workload, technician skill level.
- Do not assign High or Emergency priority jobs to Junior technicians without a Senior
  available for remote support.
- Send an ETA text to the customer when the technician is dispatched.
- Re-dispatch within 10 minutes if a technician does not acknowledge an Emergency job.

## 4. On-Call Rotation

- On-call shifts run **6 PM – 7 AM** weekdays and **all day Saturday/Sunday**.
- On-call schedule is published every Friday by 3 PM for the following week.
- On-call technician receives a $50/day on-call stipend regardless of whether they are dispatched.
- If an on-call tech is dispatched between 10 PM and 6 AM, they receive an additional
  $25 per hour on top of standard after-hours rate.

**On-call acknowledgment rule:** Tech must acknowledge the dispatch app notification within
15 minutes. If unresponsive, the dispatcher escalates to the next tech on the rotation list,
then notifies the Service Manager.

## 5. Rescheduling & Cancellation Handling

| Scenario                           | Policy                                                      |
|------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Customer cancels > 24 hours ahead  | No charge; slot released                                    |
| Customer cancels < 24 hours ahead  | $49 cancellation fee applies (waived for emergencies)       |
| Tech no-shows (our fault)          | Immediate reschedule + $50 service credit to customer       |
| Customer not home at arrival       | Tech waits 10 min, documents in app, marks "Attempted"     |
| Same-day reschedule request        | Accommodate if capacity allows; no fee                      |

---

## 6. Parts Ordering & Hold Jobs

When a job cannot be completed due to a missing part:
1. Close job as **"Incomplete – Parts Pending"** in the app.
2. Log the required part number, supplier, and estimated lead time.
3. Dispatcher creates a follow-up job automatically linked to the original.
4. Customer is notified via automated text with estimated return date.
5. Part arrives → dispatcher assigns the follow-up job within 24 hours.

Parts on backorder > 7 days must be escalated to the Service Manager to identify
an alternate supplier or equivalent part.

---

## 7. Multi-Technician Jobs

Jobs requiring two technicians (e.g., 200A panel work, large equipment replacement):
- Must be flagged as **"Two-Tech Required"** at the time of scheduling.
- Both technicians' time is billed at their respective rates.
- Lead technician is responsible for the job record and customer communication.
- Junior technicians may only serve as second technician; they cannot be job lead on
  two-tech assignments.
