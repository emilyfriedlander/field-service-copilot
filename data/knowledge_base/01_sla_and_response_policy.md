# Service Level Agreement & Response Policy
**Apex Home Services — Internal Operations Document**
*Effective: January 1, 2024 | Owner: Operations Manager*

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines response time commitments, escalation triggers, and resolution
standards for all field service work orders across HVAC, Plumbing, and Electrical trades.

---

## 2. Priority Definitions & Response Windows

| Priority    | Trigger Criteria                                                         | Response Time | Resolution Target |
|-------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Emergency** | No heat/AC in extreme weather, active water leak, total power loss, safety hazard | ≤ 2 hours     | Same day          |
| **High**    | HVAC not cooling/heating (mild weather), hot water failure, partial power loss  | ≤ 4 hours     | Same day          |
| **Medium**  | Reduced HVAC efficiency, slow drains, single outlet failure              | ≤ 24 hours    | Within 48 hours   |
| **Low**     | Maintenance, upgrades, non-urgent installs                               | ≤ 48 hours    | Within 5 business days |

**Extreme weather thresholds:** Outdoor temp > 95°F or < 32°F automatically upgrades any
HVAC no-cool/no-heat ticket to Emergency.

---

## 3. First-Time Fix (FTF) Standards

- **Target FTF Rate:** ≥ 75% across all trades (measured monthly)
- **Senior Technicians:** ≥ 82% FTF expected
- **Mid-Level Technicians:** ≥ 72% FTF expected
- **Junior Technicians:** ≥ 58% FTF expected

A job is considered *not* first-time-fixed if any of the following apply:
- A return visit is required within 30 days for the same reported issue
- Job is closed "Incomplete" or "Pending Revisit"
- Customer reports unresolved issue within 7 days

---

## 4. Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) Requirements

CSAT surveys are sent automatically within 4 hours of job completion.
Scale: 1–5 stars.

| Threshold             | Action Required                                              |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Score of 1–2          | Service manager calls customer within 24 hours; root cause logged |
| Score of 3            | Team lead reviews job notes; tech coaching if pattern detected |
| Score of 4–5          | No action required; positive feedback shared in weekly standup |

**Monthly CSAT targets:**
- Company-wide average: ≥ 4.2
- Individual technician average: ≥ 3.8 (below triggers performance plan)

---

## 5. Revisit Policy

- All revisits within 30 days of a completed job are **warranty callbacks** — no charge to customer.
- Technician dispatched for a warranty callback must be the *same* technician who performed
  the original work, unless unavailable or customer requests otherwise.
- Revisits are tracked separately from new jobs and excluded from revenue KPIs but included
  in FTF calculations.

---

## 6. Escalation Triggers (Auto-Escalate to Service Manager)

The following conditions require immediate notification to the on-call Service Manager:

1. Any Emergency ticket not acknowledged within 30 minutes of creation
2. Any job open > 48 hours without a status update
3. Three or more CSAT scores of 1 or 2 from the same technician in a 30-day period
4. Parts cost exceeding $1,500 on a single work order
5. Any job involving potential code violation or safety hazard
6. Customer requests to speak with a manager

---

## 7. After-Hours Coverage

- Emergency service available 24/7/365
- After-hours jobs incur a **$75 after-hours surcharge** applied automatically
- On-call rotation is set weekly; technician must acknowledge within 15 minutes or
  dispatch falls to next on the rotation list
