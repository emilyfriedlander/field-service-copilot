# Technician Field Handbook
**Apex Home Services — Internal Operations Document**
*Effective: January 1, 2024 | Owner: Field Operations Lead*

---

## 1. Before You Leave the Shop

- Confirm your day's job queue in the dispatch app; acknowledge each job.
- Stock van with trade-specific consumables (see Parts Inventory Checklist by trade).
- Complete the **daily vehicle inspection** form in the app before departure.
- Ensure your tablet is charged and synced; all job forms must be submitted digitally.

---

## 2. Arrival Protocol

1. **Call ahead** when 15 minutes out — residential customers require arrival notification.
2. Park in driveway or designated area; never block neighbor driveways or fire hydrants.
3. Put on **boot covers** before entering any home. No exceptions.
4. Present ID badge and confirm the customer's name and job type before starting work.
5. Walk the customer through what you plan to do; get verbal confirmation before proceeding
   on any job over $300.

---

## 3. Diagnosis & Documentation Standards

- Photograph the equipment **before** touching anything. Upload to the job record immediately.
- Document all visible defects, model numbers, serial numbers, and install dates.
- If a safety hazard is found (e.g., cracked heat exchanger, double-tapped breaker, active
  leak near electrical), **stop work**, notify the customer, and call the Service Manager
  before proceeding.
- All diagnosis notes must be entered in the app using plain language the customer can understand.

---

## 4. Parts & Pricing Authorization

| Parts Cost Range | Authorization Required        |
|------------------|-------------------------------|
| $0 – $299        | Technician self-authorized     |
| $300 – $999      | Customer verbal approval + app entry |
| $1,000 – $1,499  | Customer written approval via app signature |
| $1,500+          | Service Manager approval required before ordering |

- Never authorize work verbally without logging it in the app.
- If a part is not on the price book, call dispatch for pricing before quoting the customer.

---

## 5. Job Completion Checklist

- [ ] Test equipment/repair under normal operating conditions
- [ ] Clean up all debris, packaging, and old parts
- [ ] Show the customer the completed work and explain what was done
- [ ] Review the invoice line-by-line with the customer before collecting payment
- [ ] Collect payment or confirm financing terms before leaving
- [ ] Close the job in the app with status, hours worked, and parts used
- [ ] Upload all post-work photos to the job record
- [ ] Inform customer a satisfaction survey will be sent within 4 hours

---

## 6. Trade-Specific Safety Rules

### HVAC
- Lock out / tag out before opening any electrical panel on air handling units.
- Never recharge refrigerant without verifying the system leak rate first.
- Confirm proper airflow CFM after any duct work or coil cleaning.

### Plumbing
- Shut off the main water supply before cutting any pipe.
- Verify water heater pressure relief valve operation on all water heater jobs.
- Run water 5 minutes post-repair and inspect for leaks before leaving.

### Electrical
- Always verify circuits are de-energized with a non-contact tester before working.
- Do not work on panels over 200A without a second technician present.
- All new outlet installations require a GFCI test before closing the job.

---

## 7. Uniform & Professionalism Standards

- Wear the company uniform shirt; it must be clean and untorn.
- No smoking, vaping, or use of personal devices in customer homes.
- Do not discuss other customers, company financials, or job pricing strategies with customers.
- If a customer becomes hostile or threatening, disengage politely and call dispatch immediately.

---

## 8. Incident Reporting

Any of the following must be reported to dispatch within 1 hour of occurrence:

- Vehicle accident (any severity)
- Property damage caused by tech
- Personal injury (tech or customer)
- Customer complaint escalation
- Discovered gas leak or fire risk
